# TCS_Retail_Data-Analysis
This Power BI project analyzes worldwide sales patterns to uncover revenue trends, customer purchase behavior, top-performing markets, and monthly/quarterly insights.  It is designed for business teams to make fast, data-driven decisions using an interactive and dynamic dashboard.

# Objectives
Analyze Total Revenue, Quantity Sold & Product Performance
Identify Top/Bottom Countries by Sales
Explore Monthly & Quarterly Trends (2011)
Understand Customer Purchase Patterns
Highlight Top & Bottom 10 Products
Visualize global sales using a Geo Map

# Key Insights
## Country Performance
Netherlands, EIRE, Germany, France, Australia are top revenue contributors.
Brazil, RSA, Czech Republic, Bahrain, Saudi Arabia show minimal revenue.

## Revenue & Sales Trends
Strongest performance seen in Q3 & Q4.
Peak months: October, September, November.

## Customer Behavior
Customer IDs 14646, 18102, 17450 generate the highest revenue.

## Product Analysis
POST, M, 22423, 23084 are top-selling products.
Several bottom products show very low sales, indicating dead stock.
